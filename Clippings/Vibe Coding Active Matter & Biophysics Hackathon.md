## The Vision

This isn't your typical ML hackathon. Instead of working we datasets, we want to leverage LLM to vibe code scientific simulations. We provide simulation tools in active matter for you to model **collective behavior**, from robotic swarms to living systems and social agents.

"Role of LLM: To lower the entry barrier for students with little programming background, enabling them to run sophisticated simulations and focus on scientific exploration."

### Bio Sciences/Engineering

Enhance quantitative skills and see the math behind living and complex systems.

### Physical Sciences/Data Science

Apply modeling to living systems and self-organization.

### The Ideal Team

2-4 students. A hybrid mix of brains with diverse background (Bio/Physics/Data Science/Engineering) is highly encouraged.

## Challenge Format

We will use LLM to vibe code classic biophysics and active matter model such as phase separation, pattern formation and active Brownian particles to simulate diverse phenomena ranging from biological flocks, living cells and tissues, ecological structures to traffic jams and voter distribution.

### Example Project

How do thousands of independent agents reach a consensus of motion? The Toner-Tu model is the "hydrodynamics of flocking." It describes how simple local interactions lead to massive, coherent collective motion—breaking symmetry without a leader.

"From macroscopic birds to microscopic cytoskeleton filaments, the same laws of physics apply."
